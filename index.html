How I work

Build. Measure. Document. Iterate.

I treat projects like engineering systems rather than isolated demos: requirements first, component selection from datasheets, schematic and mechanical design, verification, physical bring-up, and documentation.

My goal is to understand why a design works—not just reproduce a tutorial— and turn that understanding into reusable design habits.

About

Broad by design.

My work sits at the intersection of electrical engineering, computer engineering, and mechanical design. I’m especially interested in semiconductors, computer architecture, embedded electronics, robotics, and the process of turning an idea into manufactured hardware.

I also bring hands-on electrical experience with power distribution, breakers, HVAC-related systems, single-line diagrams, three-phase systems, and P&ID documentation—experience that keeps my circuit work grounded in real physical systems.
