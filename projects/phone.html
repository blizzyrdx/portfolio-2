Smart Calendar

A staged project that uses a Raspberry Pi to make a smart calendar I can use to monitor my schedule.

Focus Embedded Systems
Tools Linux · C++ · OnShape · KiCad
Status In development

Why a Smart Calendar?

I’m building a smart calendar monitor because, after reading Atomic Habits, I became interested in the idea of making good habits as easy and accessible as possible. Instead of constantly checking my phone or trying to remember what I need to do, I want a dedicated display that always shows my schedule, whether I’m waking up in the morning, getting back from school, working on projects, or preparing for the next day. By keeping my schedule visible, the goal is to reduce the effort it takes to stay organized and make following a routine feel automatic. This project will also give me more hands-on experience with the Raspberry Pi, allowing me to improve my skills with Linux, programming, displays, APIs, and hardware-software integration while building something I can actually use every day.
